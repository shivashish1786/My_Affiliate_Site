## What this book is about

*Atomic Habits* argues that lasting change comes from small, consistent improvements rather than dramatic overhauls. If you get just 1% better each day, the gains compound over time.

## Key ideas

- **Focus on systems, not goals.** Goals set a direction; systems of daily habits are what actually move you.
- **Make it obvious, attractive, easy and satisfying.** The author turns habit-building into four simple levers you can adjust.
- **Identity drives behaviour.** Instead of "I want to run a marathon", think "I am a runner" and let small actions prove it.
- **Design your environment.** Reduce friction for good habits and add friction for bad ones.

## Who should read it

Anyone who has tried to build a routine and quit after a week. It is easy to read and full of everyday examples.

## My take

A friendly, practical read. The ideas are simple, and that is the point: they are easy to start today.
