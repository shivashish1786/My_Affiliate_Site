## What this book is about

*Deep Work* makes the case that the ability to concentrate without distraction is both increasingly valuable and increasingly rare, and that you can train it on purpose.

## Key ideas

- **Deep work creates value.** Hard, focused effort produces better output in less time than scattered multitasking.
- **Schedule it.** Block out fixed time for focus and protect it like an important meeting.
- **Embrace boredom.** Constantly reaching for your phone weakens your ability to focus.
- **Quit the shallow stuff.** Cut or limit email, social media and low-value tasks.

## Who should read it

Students, writers, developers and anyone whose work depends on thinking clearly.

## My take

Some advice is easier for people with flexible schedules, but the core idea works for almost everyone.
